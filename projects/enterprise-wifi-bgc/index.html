Enterprise Wi-Fi & Network Deployment — BGC, Taguig City

A sanitized overview of an enterprise network and wireless deployment focused on reliable connectivity, scalable coverage, centralized administration, and controlled guest access.

OVERVIEW

Enterprise connectivity treated as one coordinated system.

The project combined perimeter security, managed switching, enterprise wireless, and multi-provider connectivity into a coordinated infrastructure deployment. Public details are intentionally generalized to protect the client and the production environment.

OBJECTIVE

Reliable business connectivity with room to scale.

The objective was to establish a stable network foundation that could support business connectivity, managed wireless access, guest services, and future operational growth while keeping administration practical.

SCOPE

From planning through commissioning.

  • Network planning and implementation coordination
  • Switching and uplink integration
  • Firewall and internet-edge integration
  • Enterprise wireless deployment
  • Guest connectivity architecture
  • Testing, validation, and commissioning

SOLUTION APPROACH

Layered, manageable, and operationally practical.

The design separated edge connectivity, switching, wireless management, and guest access responsibilities so each layer could be administered and validated independently. Multi-provider internet connectivity was incorporated at the edge to support continuity and resilience.

TECHNOLOGIES

Enterprise platforms selected for the deployment.

Fortinet Firewall Aruba Switching UniFi Enterprise Wireless Dual ISP Connectivity Guest Network Architecture

IMPLEMENTATION METHODOLOGY

Plan, integrate, validate.

Implementation followed a staged sequence: confirm the design, prepare device and connectivity dependencies, integrate the wired and wireless layers, verify management visibility, then test user and guest connectivity before commissioning.

TESTING & COMMISSIONING

Validation focused on real operating paths.

Commissioning covered connectivity, switching paths, wireless availability, guest access behavior, management visibility, and internet-path validation.

Exact addressing, VLAN identifiers, device quantities, security policies, building identity, and other client-sensitive details are intentionally omitted from the public case study.

OUTCOME

A structured foundation for business connectivity.

The completed deployment established a centrally manageable network and wireless foundation designed for reliable business use, controlled guest connectivity, and future operational expansion.
